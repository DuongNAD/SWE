PRACTICE EXAM 2: E-COMMERCE GROCERY PLATFORM
I. CASE STUDY
FreshCart is a regional supermarket chain looking to expand its business by launching an online
grocery delivery platform. Currently, customers can only shop in-store. The new FreshCart
Delivery Platform (FDP) aims to allow users to order groceries from their homes and have them
delivered within specific time slots.
The system will cater to four primary user groups. Customers will browse categories, add items
to their digital carts, select delivery windows, and pay for their orders securely. Shoppers (store
staff) will receive order packing lists on their mobile tablets, scan items as they pack them, and
flag out-of-stock substitutions. Delivery Drivers will view optimized delivery routes, capture
delivery proof (photos or signatures), and update order status to 'Delivered'. Store Managers will
monitor daily order volumes, update product pricing, and handle customer refund requests.
Because of the competitive nature of online grocery shopping, the system's performance is
critical. It must support up to 5,000 concurrent shoppers during weekend peaks without
crashing. The checkout process must be integrated with a secure third-party payment gateway
(Stripe). All web traffic must use TLS 1.3. Additionally, the mobile app for Shoppers and Drivers
must be able to function offline temporarily if the Wi-Fi or cellular signal drops inside the
supermarket or apartment building elevators.
