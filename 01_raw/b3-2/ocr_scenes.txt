I. CASE STUDY
"SwiftMart" is a rapidly growing online grocery platform currently struggling with a manual
backend that leads to frequent delivery delays. The company has commissioned an Integrated
Fulfillment System" (IFS) to digitize its operations. Customers use the web and mobile
platform to browse products, manage their shopping carts, and process payments. A unique
feature is the "Subscription Mode," where customers can schedule recurring weekly deliveries;
the system automatically generates these orders every Monday. Delivery Riders use a
dedicated mobile interface to accept delivery assignments, navigate to customer locations
using integrated maps, and update the order status to "Delivered." Warehouse Staff are
responsible for inventory management, including updating stock levels and marking items as
"Damaged" or "Out of Stock." When inventory for a high-demand item falls below a certain
threshold, the system automatically triggers a Restock Alert" to the Procurement Team.
Managers oversee the platform by viewing real-time sales analytics, managing promotional
discount codes, and handling customer refund requests for missing items.

Service API. The system must handle up to 5,000 transactions per hour with a sub-2-second
response time for checkout. Security is paramount; all payment data must be PCI-DSS
